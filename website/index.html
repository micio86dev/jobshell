Jobshell

Find the jobs that match your CV.

Jobshell reads your CV, builds your profile with AI and ranks job postings by how well they fit you, showing the reasons behind every match.

Download for macOSApple Silicon (M1 and later) Download for WindowsWindows 10 and later Download for LinuxAppImage and .deb

Builds are not code-signed yet. macOS blocks the first launch: follow the macOS install guide. Windows SmartScreen may show a warning too. The source code is public on GitHub.

What it does

  • Upload your CVPDF, .txt or .md. The file stays on your computer.
  • An AI-built profileJobshell fills in your profile from the CV and flags the fields still missing.
  • Ranked job postingsEach posting gets a match score and the reasons for it, so you can see why it ranks where it does.
  • Your keys stay safeAPI keys are kept in the operating system keychain and read only by the app.
  • English, Italian and SpanishThe app interface is available in all three languages and follows your system language.

Status

Jobshell is at version 0.1.0. The macOS build is the one tested so far; Windows and Linux builds are produced by the release workflow and are still being verified. See the changelog for what is in each version.
